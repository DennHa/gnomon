Your way

Fits the way you already work.

Some people clock in and sort out the hours at the end of the day or the week. Others time every task as it happens. Gnomon is good at both: a timesheet that shows what's still unassigned, or a timer that switches with one click. And your project time always fits the hours you were actually at work.

Easy

Two seconds, from any app.

Press ⌃⌥P wherever you are, type a few letters of the project, press Enter: the timer runs. Switch to a call or a workshop without stopping the clock. Your planned projects for the day are one click away.

Set and forget

Set it up once. Then forget it's there.

Gnomon sits quietly in the menu bar. Forgot to clock out? It closes the day at midnight and suggests when you really left. Added an entry outside your hours? Your workday adjusts. It only reminds you when something is actually missing.

Peace of mind

Always know where you stand.

Every workday counts against your own schedule: part time, a free Friday, vacation and sick days included. The overtime balance is simply right, month by month, so "how many hours am I up?" takes one glance.

Every device

On your Mac, on your phone.

Clock in on the way to work, correct a time on the train, start a timer from the couch. The phone app stays in sync with your Mac.
